Privacy Policy – Dead Zone

Developer: Veldenix · Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Dead Zone (the "Game") on Android or iOS, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is free to play and is supported by advertising.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or any account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your game progress (coins, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads (a banner at the bottom of the screen and optional rewarded video ads). Our advertising partners collect device information, such as your device's advertising ID, to deliver and measure those ads.
  • You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads at any time in the Game's settings.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings (for example coins, upgrades, sound volume and your ad choice) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game, or using "Reset All Progress" in the settings, deletes it.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC) to show ads. Google may show ads from Google and from other advertisers and ad networks that work with Google.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google and these advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking);
  • IP address, from which an approximate (country or city level) location may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size, app version and network type;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and whether a rewarded video was completed.

This information is used to show ads, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud, measure ad performance and, if you allow it, show ads based on your interests. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you personally; we only see aggregated reports (for example total ad views and earnings).

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, the Game shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads and choose your settings. You can change your choice any time under Settings → "Ad privacy options". If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • iOS tracking: on iOS, the Game asks for permission before your IDFA can be used for tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some devices).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you only watch a rewarded video when you choose to (for example to revive or double your coins).

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help you request deletion from our ad partners.

6. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise these choices through the consent form and Settings → "Ad privacy options" (where offered), and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, requests about ad data are handled by Google; contact us and we will help you reach them. The legal basis for personalized ads is your consent, and for non-personalized ads our legitimate interest in funding the free Game.

7. Data security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies. Data sent between the Game and ad partners is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds features or partners. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
